About the department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering at FISAT combines classroom learning, laboratory practice, and collaborative projects to help students build strong computing skills. The department was established in 2002 and offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and research pathways.

We encourage curiosity, clear problem-solving, and responsible use of technology.
